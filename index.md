# Ishan Shah

**Cybersecurity and Computer Science Educator | Software, Web, and Application Developer**

![Ishan Shah's GitHub profile image](https://github.com/ishu13.png)

Welcome to my portfolio. I am an Information Technology and Cybersecurity instructor at Turtle Mountain College in North Dakota. 
My work brings together classroom teaching, software development, cybersecurity, and an interest in data science and emerging technologies.

I enjoy building tools that solve real problems, explaining technical concepts in a practical way, and exploring how technology can support students, organizations, and communities. 

## About Me

I hold a **Master of Science in Computer Science** and have more than **14 years of experience** across information technology, software development, database systems, automation, and cybersecurity. 
Before moving into full-time teaching, I spent more than 14 years at **Autozone in California**, working on database-backed applications, automation tools, and internal systems.

Much of that work focused on improving large-scale data processing and publishing workflows.
I developed and supported applications, worked through complicated technical issues, helped lead projects, and looked for ways to make systems faster, more accurate, and easier to maintain.
Those experiences continue to shape how I approach technology and how I teach it.

Today, I combine that industry experience with hands-on teaching.
I want students to understand not only how a system works, but also why certain design choices matter, what can go wrong, and how to investigate and fix problems.

## Teaching and Education

At **Turtle Mountain College**, I teach and support courses across several areas:

### Cybersecurity

My cybersecurity teaching focuses on practical skills and decision-making. 
Topics include network defense, database and application security, security operations, cloud security, risk assessment, and incident response. 
I use activities such as reviewing system configurations, investigating security weaknesses, analyzing logs, and discussing how organizations can lower their risks.

### Computer Science and Information Technology

I also teach computer science and IT-related subjects, including operating systems, Linux administration, Windows Server, hardware, databases, virtualization, and technical problem-solving. 
I aim to connect classroom lessons with the kinds of systems students may encounter in the workplace.

### Business and Technology

My teaching also extends to business and technology topics, including e-business and IT project management. 
I am interested in how businesses plan technology projects, manage resources, evaluate risks, and make decisions that affect their operations.

### Student Projects and Hands-On Learning

I believe students learn best when they can test an idea themselves. 
In coursework and capstone activities, I emphasize practical projects, documentation, troubleshooting, communication, and responsible handling of data. 
I also encourage students to ask questions early and explain what they tried when something does not work.

## Professional and Technical Experience

### Software and Application Development

I have experience building and maintaining software applications and internal tools. My work has included application logic, database integration, troubleshooting, automation, and improvements to existing systems. I am interested in both the technical work of development and the larger question of whether a tool genuinely solves the problem it was built for.

### Website Development

I enjoy designing and developing websites that are clear, useful, and easy to navigate. 
My interests include front-end development, responsive layouts, user experience, and connecting websites with applications or data when needed.

### Databases and Automation

Database-backed applications and automation have been a major part of my professional work. 
At Autozone, I worked on systems that supported large amounts of information and helped make data processing and publishing more consistent. 
I am especially interested in reducing manual work while protecting data quality.

### Cybersecurity and Risk Assessment

My cybersecurity interests include identifying weaknesses, evaluating the impact of threats, applying reasonable safeguards, and testing whether security controls work.
I am interested in risk assessments that lead to practical improvements, rather than reports that simply list problems.

## My Approach

I value practical learning, clear communication, and continuous improvement. Whether I am building software, teaching a class, or exploring a research idea, I try to ask a few simple questions: What problem are we solving? Who benefits from the solution? How will we know it works? And what risks are we creating along the way?
