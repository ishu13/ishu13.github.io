## Research Interests

I am interested in how Tribal Colleges and communities can use data science and cloud technologies while maintaining meaningful control over their data. I would like to explore approaches to secure data sharing, Indigenous Data Sovereignty, and collaboration between institutions.

Questions I hope to explore include:

1. How can smaller colleges introduce data science through existing courses?
2. How can communities collaborate on research without giving up control of sensitive data?
3. How can cybersecurity practices make research data safer and more useful?
